Affiliate Disclosure

We believe in complete transparency regarding how our publication is funded and our rigorous commitment to uncompromised editorial objectivity.

1. Regulatory Compliance (FTC, CMA & ACCC)

In compliance with the United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines (16 CFR Part 255), the United Kingdom Competition and Markets Authority (CMA), and the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC), please note:

GlowMedix participates in affiliate marketing programs. When readers click on product links, comparison table buttons, or curated product cards on GlowMedix.com and make a purchase on a merchant website, GlowMedix may earn an affiliate referral commission.

This occurs at zero additional cost to you. The price you pay is identical whether you use our referral link or navigate directly to the merchant.

2. How Affiliate Links Work on GlowMedix

Our website connects readers with trusted beauty retailers including Sephora, Cult Beauty, YesStyle, Nordstrom, Space NK, and Ulta Beauty.

Affiliate links utilize standard browser tracking cookies to attribute referral credit. These cookies do not capture sensitive personal data or financial details; they merely tell the retailer that a visitor arrived via GlowMedix.

3. Total Editorial Independence

Our editorial process remains strictly separated from commercial considerations:

  • No Paid Reviews: We do not accept payment to write positive or biased reviews. If an ingredient formulation is subpar or overpriced, we state so directly.
  • Formulation First: Products are recommended based on active ingredient percentages, clinical trial literature, pH stability, and real-world performance.
  • PR Samples: When products are provided as complimentary PR samples by beauty brands, this is explicitly identified in the respective article. Providing a sample never guarantees coverage or positive praise.
