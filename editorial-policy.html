Editorial Integrity

Editorial Policy & Standards

Our commitment to evidence-based skincare journalism, clinical literature evaluation, and transparent correction procedures.

1. Evidence-Based Research Standards

At GlowMedix, every beauty guide, ingredient deep-dive, and comparison begins with peer-reviewed scientific literature. When discussing active ingredients—such as retinoids, ascorbic acid, azelaic acid, or ceramides—our writers prioritize published dermatological and cosmetic science journals.

We examine the difference between in-vitro laboratory studies and in-vivo human clinical trials, consistently clarifying realistic expectations for our readers.

2. Non-Medical and Informational Disclaimer

GlowMedix is an editorial and discovery website. We do not offer personal medical advice, diagnose medical skin disorders, or replace face-to-face consultations with certified dermatologists or doctors.

Readers experiencing persistent skin inflammation, suspected allergic contact dermatitis, cystic conditions, or atypical moles must seek direct care from licensed healthcare professionals.

3. Product Review & Testing Integrity

When testing cosmetic formulations, we assess:

  • Formulation Stability & pH: Verifying whether actives sit within biologically functional pH ranges.
  • Sensory & Wear Performance: Checking for stickiness, pilling under makeup, oxidation, and optical finish.
  • Skin Type Suitability: Highlighting potential irritants such as synthetic fragrances or high concentrations of drying alcohols.

4. Corrections and Updates Policy

We are dedicated to accuracy. As cosmetic science evolves, product formulas are updated by manufacturers, or new regulatory findings emerge, we routinely revise our articles.

If you notice a factual discrepancy or an outdated ingredient list, we welcome your feedback. Please email our editorial desk with the article URL and supporting details.
