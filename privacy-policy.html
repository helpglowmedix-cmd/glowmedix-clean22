Trust & Privacy

Privacy Policy

GlowMedix is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how information is collected, used, and secured across our digital publication.

1. Information We Collect

GlowMedix operates primarily as an informational and editorial resource. We collect minimal personal data:

  • Newsletter Subscribers: When you voluntary subscribe to our editorial dispatch, we collect only your email address. We do not sell, rent, lease, or distribute subscriber addresses to third-party advertisers.
  • Contact Inquiries: If you reach out to our team via our contact form or our official inbox (help@glowmedix.com), we retain your message and email solely to provide customer support and reply to editorial requests.
  • Technical Log Data: Like standard web hosts, our server infrastructure collects anonymous traffic telemetry including IP addresses, browser variants, referring URLs, and timestamps to diagnose technical faults and optimize page loading performance.

2. Cookies and Aggregated Analytics

We use privacy-conscious aggregated analytics tools to evaluate reading trends and improve content navigation. These tools deploy anonymized cookies that do not personally identify visitors.

You can configure your web browser to block or delete cookies at any time through your browser's security settings without impacting your ability to browse articles on GlowMedix.com.

3. Third-Party Links & Affiliate Partners

Our articles feature links to trusted merchant platforms (such as Sephora, Cult Beauty, YesStyle, and Ulta). Once you click an outbound link and leave GlowMedix.com, your activity is governed by the respective merchant's privacy policy. We encourage readers to review the privacy notices of external websites.

4. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under international privacy frameworks including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether your personal email is held in our newsletter records.
  • Request complete deletion of your subscriber data from our records.
  • Unsubscribe instantly from communications via the automated unsubscribe link embedded at the bottom of every dispatch.

5. Contacting Our Privacy Desk

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data removal, please contact our support team at:
